PRIVACY & ACTIVITY

Control what other listeners can see. Howie (Admin) can review the most recently reported song and playing/paused state for all signed-in accounts regardless of these selections. Activity reporting is part of using this service. Details are provided below in these Privacy & Activity settings.

When enabled, other signed-in users may see that you're connected. Turning it off hides your public online indicator. It does not prevent the administrator from receiving music activity reported by your player. A connection is not proof of active use.
Everyone: other signed-in users may see your current song while your online indicator is visible. Friends only: hidden from other users until verified friendships are implemented. Nobody: hidden from other users. All three choices remain visible to Howie (Admin) through Management > Monitor.
ADMIN OVERSIGHT — ALWAYS ACTIVE WHILE SIGNED IN: Howie may view your last reported song ID/title, playing/paused state, timestamp, profile and playlist names (including private playlists) in Management > Monitor. Choosing Nobody, Friends Only, or hiding your Online indicator does not disable admin visibility. Only one latest playback snapshot per account is stored; it is not a full listening history. If you do not want your listening activity reported to the administrator, do not use the service while signed in. Your audio itself is never recorded.
